Research Experience

  • AI Research Fellow – Strathmore University, School of Computing and Engineering
    Leading research on the safety of retrieval-augmented generation systems in clinical settings. Building MatSafe, a safety-annotated benchmark corpus for maternal health retrieval spanning five clinical topics, and developing safety-focused evaluation metrics (unsafe@k, unsafe@1, safe_precision@k, safety_gap) that expose retrieval failures standard relevance metrics miss. Designed and implemented the full corpus infrastructure as a modular Python pipeline — acquisition, extraction, cleaning, chunking, tagging, embedding, retrieval, and evaluation — with a registry-based retrieval test harness.
  • MSc Research – Strathmore University
    Developed and experimentally evaluated a language-technology-based educational system for maternal and neonatal health, studying system behaviour, consistency, and failure modes under realistic conditions. Work published as a first-author paper at ICETT, co-authored with Prof. Ismail Ateya.

Teaching Experience

Supervising student projects in software engineering and applied AI, and mentoring students into international AI summer schools.
