Teaching

Teaching Fellow – Strathmore University

I teach at the School of Computing and Engineering with an emphasis on conceptual depth and engineering rigour: students should understand mechanisms, not memorise scripts. My course design connects theory, implementation, and testing practice, so that what students learn in class transfers directly to production code and to research thinking.

Courses Taught

  • Data Structures and Algorithms (ICS 1201) — four class sections
  • Mobile App Development (iOS and Android)
  • APIs and Backend Systems

Course Design and Teaching Materials

For ICS 1201, I author the full assessment cycle — continuous assessment tests, end-of-semester examinations, and marking schemes — alongside lecture materials built around verified, runnable C++ examples rather than pseudocode alone.

I also build interactive, browser-based teaching tools for algorithm concepts, including a Sorting Lab that lets students step through sorting algorithms visually and a Design Lab covering algorithm design techniques. These tools let students experiment with the mechanics of an algorithm before formalising it — the same behaviour-first approach I take in my research on system evaluation.

Mentorship

I supervise student projects in software engineering and applied AI, and I have mentored Strathmore students into international AI summer schools, supporting their applications and preparation for research environments abroad.

Certification

I am an Apple-Certified Instructor, a credential recognising instructor-level technical quality and delivery in Apple's development ecosystem, which grounds my mobile development teaching.
